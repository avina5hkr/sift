# M5: Near-duplicates

Working method: `CLAUDE.md`. Roadmap: section 5, M5. Spec: 8 C–E, 10 (tabs), 16 (fixtures).
Nothing in M5 deletes or writes anything on the phone or in a backup folder.

## Features, in build order

1. **Perceptual hashes** (`analysis/phash.py`, stage C, analysis_version 2): dHash (9×8 grey)
   and pHash (32×32 DCT, top-left 8×8, median), 64-bit, stored signed in `content.dhash/phash`.
   Computed from the decoded image (draft mode) during indexing of each image.
2. **Near-duplicate images** (`analysis/near.py`, stage D): same kind, aspect within
   `neardup_aspect_tol`; dHash split into 9 bands (pigeonhole for distance ≤ 8), bucket by
   band value, compare pairs inside buckets, buckets over 500 get a pHash pre-filter; confirm
   when dHash ≤ 6 and pHash ≤ 8 (no embeddings until M6); union-find; group type wa_forward /
   mixed / camera_burst; keeper score +1000 camera origin, +500 camera EXIF, then pixels, then
   size, then the earlier mtime. Stored in `near_group`/`near_member`.
3. **Video fingerprints** (`analysis/video.py`, stage E): duration and 5 frame dHashes at
   10/30/50/70/90 % through AVFoundation; poster frame (50 %) as the video's thumbnail. Two
   videos group when durations differ by ≤ max(1 s, 2 %), ≥ 4 of 5 frames are within distance
   10 by position, and aspect is within 6 %. Keeper: resolution, then size. Camera videos are
   never suggested.
4. **Candidates**: tab `near` (wa_forward and mixed groups; copies suggested, pre-checked only
   when dHash ≤ 4 — cosine arrives in M6), tab `similar` (camera_burst; never pre-checked).
   Keeper locked. Same API as exact (`/api/cleanup/candidates?tab=near|similar`).
5. **Carry-overs from M4**: Camera rank only for DCIM/Camera (not every DCIM folder); WhatsApp
   documents and voice notes count as Other, not WhatsApp images.
6. **Fixtures** (`scripts/make_fixtures.py`): base images drawn with Pillow and their variants
   (50 % resize, JPEG q30, WebP, double recompress, 5 % crop, 90° rotation, brightness), and
   videos written as Motion-JPEG QuickTime files (clip, lower quality copy, 1 s shorter copy,
   unrelated clips), plus `expected.json` (groups that must match, pairs that must not).
7. **Web**: enable the Near-duplicates and Similar shots tabs with the same group rows.

## Exit

Fixture accuracy tests pass: every resize/recompress/WebP variant groups with its original
(recall ≥ 0.98 over the listed must-match pairs) and no two different base images ever group;
re-encoded and trimmed videos group, unrelated clips do not. Full suite, lint, web, e2e green.
One whole-branch review.

## Assumptions

- C1. Videos are read with AVFoundation (built into macOS) instead of bundled ffmpeg/ffprobe:
  no extra binaries or licences. Fixture videos are Motion-JPEG `.mov` files written by our own
  small writer, so tests need no external tool.
- C2. Crops and 90° rotations may be missed (spec allows it); they are listed in the fixtures as
  "may match", not "must match".
- C3. Until embeddings exist (M6), the stricter limits apply: dHash ≤ 6, pHash ≤ 8.
- C4. Near and similar candidates use the same group-row UI as exact duplicates; no new design.
